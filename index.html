🎒 What to Pack

Each student is allowed one 20kg suitcase and one small backpack that fits under the seat. Do not overpack.

🧥 Ski Outer Layers

  • Ski jacket (waterproof)
  • Ski pants / salopettes
  • Ski gloves
  • Ski goggles or sunglasses
  • Hat
  • Snood / neck warmer

🧣 Mid & Base Layers

  • 3–4 thermal pants & tops (base layer)
  • 2–3 fleeces or ¼-zip tops (mid layer)
  • Everyday tops / t-shirts
  • Ski socks — 5 pairs minimum

👟 Footwear

  • Runners / trainers (for evenings)
  • Sliders or Crocs (hotel / après ski)
  • Ski boots are provided on the mountain

🧴 Personal Care

  • Sun cream / high SPF moisturiser
  • Lip balm (UV-protecting)
  • Toiletries as normal
  • Swimwear & swim hat (pool)

🔌 Tech & Essentials

  • Phone charger & EU adapter
  • Power pack / portable charger
  • Headphones (NO Bluetooth speakers)
  • Travel pillow
  • Books / Kindle
  • Snacks for the journey

💊 Medical & Documents

  • Passport (teachers will collect on the day)
  • EHIC card
  • Any medication — clearly labelled, inform teachers of dosage & storage
  • Cash for lunches on the mountain (€12–€15/day)
  • Extra spending money

⚠️ One per room: One hairdryer/straightener and one adapter/extension plug per room — coordinate with your roommates before packing.

🏥 EHIC Card & Insurance

All students are fully insured through Topflight. An EHIC card is also strongly recommended.

European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) — While not essential, we strongly recommend every student has one. It makes visiting hospitals and doctors in Austria significantly easier and faster. It is free to get — apply well in advance of the trip.

🪪 Apply for Your EHIC Card

Groups travelling from Ireland should apply at:

www.ehic.ie — it's free and takes a few minutes online.

Apply as early as possible — cards can take a few weeks to arrive.

✈️ Travel Information

Important reminders for the journey.

🛂 Passports

  • Irish passports must be valid for at least 3 months from the date of travel
  • Passports will be collected by teachers on the day — bring yours to Shannon
  • Non-EU passport holders may need a visa — check in advance

🧳 Luggage Allowance

  • One 20kg suitcase per student
  • One small backpack that fits under the seat
  • Do not overpack — you will be able to buy anything you forget in a local shop on day 2

🔒 Airport Security

  • If wearing a hoodie, you will be asked to remove it at security — wear a top underneath
  • Liquids must be under 100ml in a clear bag
  • Meeting point and time TBC — details to follow closer to departure

📱 Phones & Devices

  • Phones may be used during the day
  • Phones are collected at 11:00 pm every evening and returned at wake-up
  • Students who need to contact home during lights-out will be facilitated by teachers
  • Bluetooth speakers are not permitted
