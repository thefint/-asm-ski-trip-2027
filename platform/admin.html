🎥 Videos

Add past trip highlight videos to your site by pasting a Google Drive or YouTube link.

⭐ Recommended: Google Drive — free, works straight from your phone, and Google prepares the video for smooth playback.
  1. Upload the video to Google Drive (the Drive app on your phone works).
  2. Tap Share → General access → change it to Anyone with the link (Viewer).
  3. Tap Copy link and paste it below.
If the link isn't set to Anyone with the link, parents will see "You need access" instead of the video. Newly uploaded videos can take a few minutes to show a preview while Google processes them.

📝 Forms for Parents & Students (optional)

Link a Google Form (or any online form) for parents or students to fill in, e.g. a rooming list, medical form or consent form. Each one appears on your site with an "Open form" button. Leave this empty if you don't use forms.

In Google Forms, press Send → the link icon 🔗 → Copy. If your form only accepts responses from your school's Google accounts, parents without one won't be able to open it. Check under Settings → Responses.

📄 Policies

Add school policies for parents to read before the trip, e.g. School Tour Policy, Code of Behaviour. Paste a link (Google Drive, school website) or upload a PDF. These appear in a Policies section at the end of your site.

⚠️ GDPR & Photo Consent Reminder

Before publishing photos or videos of students on this site, you must have obtained written parental/guardian consent for each student who appears.

Hotel photos, resort images, and general scenery are fine without consent. Photos or videos that identify individual students require a signed consent form from their parent or guardian before publishing.

Under GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679), images of identifiable students are personal data. Your school is the data controller for this site and is responsible for ensuring consent is in place.
